## **At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology.**

Are you currently enrolled in an engineering program and seeking an exciting summer 2027 internship with a leading Silicon Valley technology company? Join the Systems Division Sales Team of Cadence Design Systems as an Account Technical Executive (ATX) intern, where you will work in driving adoption and growth of the Cadence Allegro PCB, IC Packaging and Sigrity solutions in North America. You will gain insight into leading-edge systems technologies, learn how a global technology company operates, and experience how sales and technical teams solve complex engineering challenges.

  
Cadence is a global leader in electronic and systems design, building on more than 35 years of computational software expertise. Through its Intelligent System Design strategy, Cadence delivers software, hardware, and intellectual property that transform silicon, systems and electronic design concepts into reality.

  
Cadence serves many of the world’s most innovative companies, enabling extraordinary electronic products—from chips and boards to complete systems—for dynamic markets including consumer electronics, hyperscale computing, 5G communications, automotive, aerospace, industrial, and healthcare.

  
At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who are committed to advancing technology and making a meaningful global impact.

**  
Where is the internship located?**  
San Jose, California  
  
**What does the internship offer?**  
Interns will participate in a three-month, cohort-based program alongside peers from across the United States. The program provides hands-on training in applications supporting engineering analysis, system design and tools; digital/analog IC design and tools; advanced verification; hardware emulation; and the application of artificial intelligence across these disciplines. Interns will also learn from technical experts and application engineers who provide design services, technical sales support, and customer support to Cadence’s most advanced customers.

**  
The cohort-based program provides experience in the following areas:**

*   Supporting pre-sales campaigns, including technical evaluations, demos and quoting inputs.
*   Learning how software adoption is managed, forecasted, and grown in a territory of large PCB (Printed Circuit Board) accounts.
*   Working with field Account Executives, Marketing and Engineering to connect Cadence solutions to customer problems and roadmap needs.
*   Understanding core PCB technology requirements for the targeted market segments.
*   Presenting findings and deliverables to the team and leadership.

**  
How long is the internship?**  
The internship is a three-month program scheduled for summer 2027.

**  
Position Requirements:**

The ideal candidate is a rising senior pursuing a bachelor’s degree in electrical or computer engineering, computer science, or a business or engineering-technology discipline with a technical focus. For exceptional candidates, master’s degree students will also be considered for this program. Strong candidates will have coursework or interest in electronics, PCB design, or semiconductor and systems design with a plus for exposure to Allegro, Mentor, Zuken or Altium. A candidate needs to be comfortable with Excel or data analysis; with Salesforce or Tableau experience considered a plus. Candidates must be able to manage multiple complex assignments and demonstrate strong communication, presentation, assessment, and analytical skills.
